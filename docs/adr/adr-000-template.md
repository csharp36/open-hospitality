# ADR-NNN: &lt;short title of the decision&gt;

- **Status:** Proposed | Accepted | Superseded by ADR-XXX
- **Date:** YYYY-MM-DD
- **Deciders:** &lt;who made the call&gt;

## Context

What forces are at play — technical, product, legal, cost? What makes this decision
necessary now? State the problem neutrally, before the choice.

## Decision

The choice, in active voice: "We will …". Be specific enough that a reader can tell
whether the codebase still honors it.

## Consequences

What becomes easier and what becomes harder as a result. Name the costs you are
accepting, not just the benefits — an honest ADR records the trade.

## Alternatives considered

Each option not chosen, and the reason it lost. "None" is rarely true; if a decision
had no alternatives it probably wasn't a decision.
