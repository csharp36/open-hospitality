# USALI Schedule Structure

## Summary Operating Statement (Hotel P&L)

```
Revenue
  - Rooms
  - Food & Beverage
  - Other Operating Departments (incl. Minor Operated Departments; minibar moved here in 12th ed.)
  - Miscellaneous Income
Total Operating Revenue

Departmental Expenses (Rooms / F&B / Other)
Total Department Income

Undistributed Operating Expenses
  - Administrative & General
  - Information & Telecommunications Systems
  - Sales & Marketing
  - Property Operations & Maintenance
  - Energy, Water & Waste  (renamed from "Utilities" in 12th ed.)
Gross Operating Profit
Management Fees
Non-Operating Income & Expenses
EBITDA
```
