# Miscellaneous Income (Schedule 4) & Guest Fees

- **Resort / facility fees → Miscellaneous Income (Sch 4).** Explicitly excluded from Room
  Revenue and from ADR. HIGH confidence (rule changed in the 11th ed.).
- **Individual no-show / cancellation** → Rooms, Other Rooms Revenue (see `rooms-schedule.md`).
- **Group cancellation / attrition** → Misc Income (Sch 4).

## Uncertain / needs-review classifications
- **Parking** — generally Misc Income; may be an Other Operated Dept (Sch 3) if run as a
  full department with its own payroll/expenses. *Needs verification.*
- **Pet fees** — no direct USALI citation found; likely Misc Income or Other Rooms Revenue
  depending on structure. *Unverified — map as LOW confidence / needs-review.*
